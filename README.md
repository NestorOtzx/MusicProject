# Music Project

## **Team Members:** Nestor Ortiz & Laura Franco

For the project, we wanted to represent the suspense during the battle, and we also wanted to find sounds that would work well together and produce the feelings mentioned above.

Initially, we downloaded an app that allowed us to simulate a piano, then we developed a melody and began to program it and arrange the layers and how we wanted them to sound.

Finally, we put the sound on top of the gameplay and tried to ensure that the sounds of the gameplay did not disappear behind the composition.
